NSM icons

Nineteen hand-drawn icons in one style: a 24 grid, one stroke weight, one moss part and one small move each. Hover any icon, or tab to it, to see it move.

Motion is off when your system asks for reduced motion.

1. In the sidebar

The sidebar at true size: 16 px icons, same rows and states as the app. The first row shows the current state. Hover any row, the settings button or the edge handle.

2. The set

Each icon at 96 px, with its idea and its motion. One grid, one stroke weight, round caps and joins. The moss part is the only colour.

nsm-home

Home

A little house. The door opens and a moss light turns on inside.

The door swings open on hover and closes when the hover ends.

nsm-dashboard

Dashboard

Three round bars and a moss dot on the tallest.

The bars drop and regrow one after another. The dot pops last.

nsm-packaging

Packaging

A friendly box. The lid lifts and a moss spark rises out.

The lid lifts and the spark rises on hover. Both settle back when the hover ends.

nsm-production

Production

A clapperboard with a moss play mark.

The clapper claps twice. The play mark ticks on the second clap.

nsm-keystone

Keystone

A stone arch. The top stone is moss, the keystone that holds the arch.

The keystone lifts, drops with a small thud and settles.

nsm-inspiration

Inspiration

A round-cornered star with a moss spark beside it.

The star turns one point and swells. The spark twinkles.

nsm-network

Network

A moss hub with three linked account nodes.

The nodes pulse one after another and the hub swells.

nsm-improvements

Improvements

Three sliders. One knob is moss.

The knobs slide to new places on hover and slide back when the hover ends.

nsm-tracking

Tracking

Two linked chain rings. One ring is moss.

The rings pull apart and snap back together.

nsm-requests

Requests

A speech bubble with a moss plus.

The plus turns a quarter and swells.

nsm-prints

Prints

A small cube, the thing being printed. The inner edges are moss.

The inner edges draw in again, like a part being built.

nsm-updates

Updates

A bell with a moss clapper.

The bell rings and the swing dies down.

nsm-settings

Settings

A gear with a moss hub ring.

The gear turns one tooth. It looks the same at the end.

nsm-collapse

Collapse sidebar

A panel edge with an arrow. The edge is moss.

The arrow nudges toward the edge and settles.

nsm-sun

Light theme

A sun with eight rays and a moss core.

The rays turn one step and the core swells.

nsm-moon

Dark theme

A crescent moon with two moss stars.

The moon tips back and returns. The stars twinkle.

nsm-stages

Stages

A board with three columns. A small moss card sits in the first.

The card hops into the second column and hops back.

nsm-more

More

Three dots. The middle one is moss.

The dots hop in a wave.

nsm-macro

Macro

Four cells. The top-right cell is moss.

The moss cell and the cell below it swap places on hover and swap back when the hover ends.

3. Told apart at 16 px

All 19 icons in one row at sidebar size, then at 48 px. Each silhouette must differ from every other one.

Keyboard: press Tab to reach an icon. Focus plays the same motion as hover.
